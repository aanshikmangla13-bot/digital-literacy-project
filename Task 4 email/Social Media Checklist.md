# Social Media Dos and Don'ts for College Students

## DO's (5 Essential Practices)

| # | Do This |
|---|---------|
| 1 | **Review privacy settings** monthly - Ensure your profiles are visible only to intended audiences |
| 2 | **Think before you post** - Consider if content could affect future job prospects |
| 3 | **Use strong, unique passwords** for each platform with a password manager |
| 4 | **Enable two-factor authentication** wherever available |
| 5 | **Post content that builds your professional brand** - Share achievements, learning, positive engagement |

 ## DON'Ts (5 Things to Avoid)

| # | Avoid This |
|---|-----------|
| 1 | **Don't share personal information** like address, phone number, or vacation plans publicly |
| 2 | **Don't post inappropriate content** - including party photos, offensive language, or controversial opinions |
| 3 | **Don't engage in online arguments** or negative comment threads |
| 4 | **Don't accept friend requests from strangers** without verifying their identity |
| 5 | **Don't overshare** - Avoid posting every moment of your daily life |

## Quick Tip
Your social media presence is your digital footprint. Future employers, internship coordinators, and academic committees may review it. Make sure what they find represents you well!
