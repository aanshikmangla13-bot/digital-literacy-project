# Cybercrime Case Study: UPI Phishing Scam Targeting College Students

## What is it?
Phishing is a cybercrime where attackers pose as legitimate organizations to trick victims into revealing sensitive information like passwords, OTPs, or bank details.

## How it Typically Happens (Step-by-Step)

**Step 1: The Bait**
The victim receives an SMS or WhatsApp message claiming: "Your bank account will be blocked. Update KYC immediately: [fake link]"

**Step 2: The Trap**
The link leads to a fake website that looks exactly like the bank's official page or a UPI payment interface.

**Step 3: Information Harvesting**
The victim enters their:
- Bank account number
- UPI PIN
- Debit card details
- Phone number

**Step 4: The Exploit**
The attacker uses this information to:
- Initiate a fraudulent transaction
- Request an OTP from the bank
- The victim receives the OTP and enters it on the fake site

**Step 5: The Loss**
Money is transferred from the victim's account within seconds.

## Who is Targeted?
- College students who frequently use UPI apps (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm)
- Elderly individuals less familiar with digital payments
- Anyone who responds to urgent-sounding messages

## Realistic Scenario
Rahul, a first-year engineering student, received a message saying his Google Pay account would be suspended unless he updated his KYC immediately. The link took him to a page identical to Google Pay. He entered his UPI PIN and within minutes, ₹8,500 was debited from his account.

## Consequences
- **Financial:** Immediate loss of money (often life savings or fees money)
- **Emotional:** Stress, anxiety, loss of trust in digital platforms
- **Academic:** Distraction from studies, need to take time to file complaints
- **Legal:** Lengthy process to recover money through cybercrime reporting
