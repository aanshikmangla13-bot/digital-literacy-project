Tool used: Canva
Date created: 29 march 2026 

My infographic covers three main topics: safe internet practices, useful digital tools for students, and email etiquette. The design uses a clean, colorful layout to make information easily digestible for my batchmates. I found it interesting to learn how visual hierarchy can guide viewers' attention to the most important information first. The most difficult part was condensing complex topics into brief, impactful points without losing essential information.
